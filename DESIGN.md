# Design System & Visual Grammar

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Design Philosophy & Identity

Globfreight's interface is built on institutional trust, maritime clarity, and operational precision. It balances high scannability for commercial freight operators with a modern, high-conversion presentation for decision-makers.

## Color Palette

- **Primary Brand**: `--logo-orange` (Vibrant Logistics Orange, `hsl(26 100% 50%)`)
- **Secondary / Deep Navy**: `--logo-navy` (Maritime Deep Navy, `hsl(212 100% 19%)`)
- **Background**: Clean crisp white (`hsl(0 0% 100%)`) with slate-950 contrast accents for footer, CTA, and stats bands.
- **Card Surfaces**: Pure white with subtle 1px border (`border-border/80`) and soft elevation shadow (`shadow-xs`).
- **Success / Ingress Accent**: Emerald green (`#10B981`) for live EDI signals and certified statuses.

## Typography & Hierarchy

- **Font Family**: Modern Graphik / Sans-serif system font stack.
- **Display Headings**: Bold, high-contrast headings with tight tracking (`tracking-tight`), natural hierarchy (H1 at 40-50px, H2 at 28-36px, H3 at 18-24px).
- **Body & Subtitles**: 14-16px with relaxed line-height for effortless reading across English and Arabic (LTR/RTL).

## Component Conventions

- **Hero Command Center**: Clean two-column layout coupling high-impact value proposition and direct CTAs with a maritime gateway terminal visual.
- **Interactive Service Tabs**: 4 core logistics services with smooth switching, 3-point feature checklist, and metric highlight cards.
- **Strategic Gateway Corridor**: Interactive port hub selector highlighting turnaround times, highway infrastructure, and customs capabilities.
- **Trust Pillars**: 3 crisp cards showcasing AEO-F certification, sub-4-hour port release, and single-dispatcher accountability.
- **Conversion CTA**: High-contrast, dark slate band with instant quote request and direct telephone dispatch links.
