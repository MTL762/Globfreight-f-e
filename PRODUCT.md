# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Importers, exporters, supply chain managers, freight forwarders, and logistics operators moving containerized freight into and through Europe via major ports (Antwerp-Bruges, Rotterdam, Zeebrugge, Hamburg).

## Product Purpose

Provide fast, reliable European freight forwarding, direct seaport customs clearance, bonded warehousing, and inland transport with transparent tracking and zero demurrage delays.

## Positioning

AEO-F accredited logistics partner offering pre-arrival direct EDI customs clearance, sub-4-hour port release windows, and bonded intermodal haulage across primary European gateways.

## Operating Context

- High-stakes international cargo clearance requiring strict compliance with Belgian, Dutch, and European Union customs regulations.
- Time-critical port operations where delays cause demurrage/detention costs.
- Cross-border multi-lingual audience (English, Arabic, European languages).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities: Seaport customs clearance (AEO-F certified), road & inland container transport, Type II bonded warehousing, fiscal representation & EU VAT deferment, real-time shipment quote requests, interactive service exploration, regulatory knowledge hub.
- Tech Stack: Next.js 15 (App router), React, Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, next-intl (English & Arabic with RTL support).
- Constraints: Maintain bilingual compatibility (English / Arabic), clean accessibility, responsive across desktop and mobile devices.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: Globfreight
- Brand Essence: Institutional trust, precision maritime & customs engineering, clear operational clarity, modern European logistics authority.
- Tone of Voice: Clear, authoritative, efficient, professional, direct.

## Evidence on Hand

- AEO-F customs accreditation & EDI direct seaport interfaces.
- Real maritime port photography (`/manus-storage/winz-hero-port_b04a3a45.jpg`, `/manus-storage/winz-customs-documents_a858a147.jpg`).
- Core service lines: Customs Clearance, Road Transport, Bonded Warehousing, Fiscal Representation.

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity Over Clutter**: Present mission-critical logistics capabilities and actions immediately without overwhelming telemetry or dense jargon.
2. **Operational Trust**: Anchor value in concrete certifications (AEO-F), speed guarantees (sub-4h clearance), and port connectivity.
3. **Frictionless Action**: Keep quotation and inquiry workflows prominent and effortless.
4. **Bilingual & Responsive Excellence**: Ensure balanced layouts, typography, and scannability across both English and Arabic (LTR/RTL).
